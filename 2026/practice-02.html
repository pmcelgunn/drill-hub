S1Offense — Hands & Routes
Coach 1 · balls, 4 cones
▶

1. 9-Spot Hands — 5 min

Pairs. Nine spots, hands only, tuck every catch. This is the habit-builder — thumbs together high, pinkies together low.

2. Over-the-Shoulder Track — 6 min

Run away, head turns on "ball," catch in stride. Alternate shoulders. Your deep threats show themselves here.

Coach it now (testing is over)

  • Fix the body-catchers today — hands out front, eyes through the ball.
  • Every catch gets tucked before the first step. Every one.

S2Defense — Pull & Pedal
Coach 2 · flags on everyone, cones at 10 yds
▶

1. Flag Pull Technique — 6 min

Breakdown, hands low, eyes on the belt. Half speed to full speed. One flag, no shirt-grabbing.

2. Backpedal & Break — 5 min

Pedal on the whistle, plant and break at 45° on the second. Chest over the toes the whole way.

Coach it now

  • Lungers from Thursday's bracket get extra breakdown reps — the fix is patience, not speed.
  • Say it every rep: eyes on the hips, never the head fake.

S3Speed & Agility
Coach 3 · speed ladder, stretch cord, cones
▶

1. Speed Ladder Series — 5 min

One-in, two-in, lateral, in-in-out-out. Fast feet, quiet feet, eyes up.

2. Resisted Starts — 6 min

Cord on, drive 10 yards against it, three reps — then one free rep that feels like flying. Full rest between; this is power, not conditioning.

Coach it now

  • Kids who stood straight up out of the combine start live at this station — the cord forces the lean the stopwatch asked for.
  • Watch shin angle on the first three steps: forward, not vertical.

S4QB School
Patrick · balls, 2 cones for a gate · QB candidates + rotating receivers
▶

Who's here

Each pod sends its QB candidates from the Position Board; everyone else in the pod rotates as centers and receivers, so the whole pod stays busy and the QBs throw to moving targets.

1. Three-Step Drop — 4 min

Snap, three quick steps, plant, throw. Ball stays at the chest, eyes downfield. 8U does one step and throw.

2. Drop & throw to live routes — 7 min

Receivers alternate two routes: 5-yard hitch and quick slant. Center snaps, QB drops, throws on time. Rotate the receiver line every rep, rotate QBs every four throws.

Coach it now

  • Throw on the plant step — waiting is a sack in this league.
  • Hitch: ball arrives as the receiver turns. Slant: lead him, chest high.
  • Centers matter here too — every rep starts with a real snap.

Scrimmage
Two fields · all coaches · flags on, pinnies out
▶

Format

Two games at once so nobody stands around: Field A — 8U plus the younger 10U pod. Field B — 12U plus the rest of 10U. Rotate every kid through offense and defense; kids playing up slot wherever the numbers need them.

Let them play. Coach between snaps, not during them.

Sharks & Minnows
Whole team · flags on · the whole field
▶

How it runs

Everyone lines up on one end line with flags on. Two sharks start in the middle. On "swim!" everybody runs to the far line; anyone whose flag gets pulled becomes a shark. Last minnow standing wins — and the last two or three survivors are always worth remembering, because surviving that chaos IS flag football.

8U gets a two-step head start each round. Run it until about 7:55.
