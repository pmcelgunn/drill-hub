Leaderboards

Ranked inside each age group only. A kid playing up in a second division shows in BOTH groups, ranked against each pool separately. An 8-year-old's dash never gets compared to a 12-year-old's.

Before you post anything. Print personal-record sheets for each kid individually. A public ranked list with names on it is how you lose the kid who finished last, and that kid is usually the one who most needs to come back Tuesday.

Position Board

Every kid's raw numbers get turned into a percentile inside their own age group, then blended by position. A score of 100 means best in their age group at what that position needs. Use it to build a shortlist, then trust your eyes over the math.

Save / Load

Rough sorting bands

Hand-timed field benchmarks for grouping kids, not published norms. Hand timing runs about two tenths faster than electronic, and these are built for hand timing. Use them to decide who needs what, never to tell a kid where they stand.
