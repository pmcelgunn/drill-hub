Combine Night

Thursday, Aug 20 · 6:00–8:00 PM
Field: ________________________
Please arrive by 5:50 so we can get everyone checked in.
8U 10U 12U All three ages, same field, same time
What tonight is: we measure where every kid is starting. Speed, jumping, catching, throwing, and one-on-ones. We write the numbers down and run the same tests again in October so your kid can see exactly how much they improved. This is not a tryout. Nobody gets cut. Positions get sorted out over the season, not tonight.
Bring
  • Water. More than you think. It's two hours of running.
  • Cleats or running shoes.
  • Flag belt, if you already have one.
  • Mouthguard, if your league requires one.

That's the whole list. Leave everything else in the car.

No names on any of it
Nobody's time gets posted with their name on it. Every kid wears a numbered pinnie, and the number is what goes on the card. The times belong to your kid and the coaches. That's all.
What your kid will actually do
  • Run a short sprint
  • Jump as far as they can from a standing start
  • Catch some passes
  • Throw some passes
  • Play one-on-one keep-away with flags
  • Finish with a team relay race

Read this part out loud to your player

"You're not trying to beat anybody tonight. Just go hard on every turn. We do all of it again in October, and that's when you get to see how much better you got."

One ask

I need two parent volunteers at every practice. Nothing technical — you'd hold a stopwatch or a clipboard.

Grab me Thursday night and give me your name. Two names is all I need.

See you at the field. — Coach Patrick
