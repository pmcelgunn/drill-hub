S28U — Stay Home & Pull Flags
Patrick · 1 ball, hoops, cones, flags on · fixes from game 1
▶

1. Zone Hoops refresher — 8 min

Two hoops, deep and short: "this grass is yours." Re-install the backside rule — ball goes away, you pursue, but you do NOT chase past the middle of the field. Name what happened Saturday: the far-left kid ran all the way to the right sideline, and the cutback lane he left behind was the touchdown.

2. Carry & Deliver (Zone Pass-Off) — 20 min

One crosser, two defenders (8U level), scripted words: "CROSSER!" ... "GOT HIM!" Nobody chases across the field — track the ball with your eyes, stay in your grass, be ready to help.

The key rep: every few plays the QB scrambles across the line — all zones dissolve and everyone swarms. Stay home while the ball is in the air; leave home the instant the QB runs. That's the whole Saturday fix in one drill.

3. Flag-Pull Angle Progression — 20 min

Three stages: Tunnel (easy two-hand pulls, grip the base, rip down, run through) → Zig-Zag (break down as the runner approaches — butt down, choppy feet, never flat-footed) → Open-Field square (body in front FIRST, force the move, then both hands to the flags).

Coach it now — straight from the game film in your head

  • Praise the kid who STAYS while the ball goes the other way. Make him the hero of the huddle.
  • DON'T LUNGE — a lunge that misses is six points. Belly button never lies.
  • Both hands, every pull. The one-hand swipes are what missed on Saturday.

S310/12 — Eyes, Feet & Full-Speed Routes
Blair · balls, cones, flags on · fixes from game 1
▶

1. Wave Drill (Break on the Point) — 12 min

4–6 kids in stance, Blair holds the ball 7 yards away. Ball raised = pedal · point = shuffle/drive that way · ball overhead = turn and run · ball tucked = "RUN!" everyone sprints a flag angle. End each set with a live throw.

This is the flat-feet fix: the drill starts when the ball moves, not two seconds later. You will run to what you look at. 12U wrinkle: add a pump-fake they must NOT bite on.

2. Defend the Seam (Safety Reads) — 18 min

Safety at his real landmark (12–15 yards, middle). Two receivers run seams, QB picks one. Safety reads the shoulders and front hip — never the eyes — breaks on the release, and high-points for the pick or a two-hand knockdown.

Rule one, every rep: stay deeper than the deepest — nobody behind you, ever. A receiver running past the safety restarts the rep. This is exactly what happened Saturday; tonight it stops.

3. Routes on Air (Tempo Grading) — 18 min

Cones at the line and at every break point. Every route at full game tempo against air, graded out loud on three things: stem at speed · break in ≤2 steps · finish with a tuck. Fail any one and the rep re-runs immediately. Two perfect reps per route per kid.

Why on air: a jogged route on air teaches jogging — and jogged routes are what the 12U kids ran Saturday. 12U self-grades: name your own worst segment before the coach does.

Coach it now — Blair's talking points

  • Safeties: eyes bounce receiver–QB–receiver. Staring at the QB is how kids ran right past us.
  • Feet moving at the snap — the kid standing still at "two-Mississippi" already lost the rep.
  • Effort IS the skill at station 3. Praise the kid who sprints a route nobody is covering.

Format

Two games at once: Field A — 8U. Field B — 10/12. Rotate every kid through offense and defense; borrow a kid across fields if the numbers get lopsided.

Carry the stations onto the field: on 8U defense, call "STAY HOME!" before the snap and swarm on every scramble. On 10/12, watch the safety's eyes and the route speed — restart a play where the routes are jogged. Praise the fixes loudly the moment they show up.

Let them play. Coach between snaps, not during them.
